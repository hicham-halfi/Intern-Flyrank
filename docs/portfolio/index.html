FlyRank / Reproducible ML audit

Content decline, without the easy answers

Problem: a content editor needs a review queue they can trust.

What I did: reproduced a forest model, separated clients across train and test, audited feature timing, and built a playbook with explicit human approval rules.

What came of it: the original feature set's Precision@20 changed from 90% to 50% after client separation. The finding is retrospective; it does not promise future accuracy or traffic lift.
